# Project context, evidence and applicability

Read the current project's instructions and `.agents/product-marketing.md` when present. Use the actual business model, customer journey, resources and permission scope. If these are absent, gather only the information needed for the task; do not require a SaaS-style intake for a local service.

Treat upstream frameworks, examples, tool names, benchmarks and platform specifications as starting points, not verified facts about this business. Check material current claims against primary documentation and the available evidence. Distinguish observations, owner reports, external estimates, hypotheses and unknowns. A psychological framework or a competitor's offer does not establish causal effects or commercial success.

For a local service, choose the actual stages (for example, inquiry, booking, payment and delivery). Do not substitute signup, trial activation, ARR, subscription churn, virality or venture-funding formulas where they do not apply. Assess capacity and uncertainty before forecasting or testing. Small samples may support qualitative learning without supporting an A/B-test conclusion.

Check actual tools and account access. Integration guides and related skills are optional references, not installed connections, requirements to buy software, or permission to run scripts. No skill grants account access or expands the user's task. Follow the project's privacy and analytics-testing rules; report delivery separately from a browser action.

Scale work to the request. Reviews remain reviews; authorised implementation can proceed without an invented additional approval gate. Publishing, outreach, spending and other external actions depend on the user's actual authorisation. Preserve customer agency and truthful claims.
